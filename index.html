01 · About the symposium

A meeting at the interface of structure, dynamics and application.

The symposium gathers experts from different fields to discuss state-of-the-art fundamental and applied research on novel liquid-crystal-related soft materials.

The programme is conceived as a compact platform for generating new research ideas, building collaborations and connecting the liquid-crystal community with HKUST’s strengths in displays, optoelectronics and soft-matter physics.

Three days of scientific exchange at the HKUST Jockey Club Institute for Advanced Study.
